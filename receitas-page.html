Monster Energy

Receita Monster Energy:

Ingredients

  • Água gaseificada
  • Açúcar
  • Xarope de Glicose
  • Taurina
  • Cafeína
  • Vitaminas B2, B3, B5, B6 e B12
  • Extrato Guaraná

Instructions

  1. Combine all ingredients in a large mixing bowl.
  2. Stir until the sugar is completely dissolved.
  3. Pour the mixture into a clean bottle and seal tightly.
  4. Refrigerate for at least 2 hours before serving.
  5. Serve chilled and enjoy your homemade Monster Energy drink!
